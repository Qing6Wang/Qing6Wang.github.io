Research

Working Papers

Moral Licensing? ESG Score Improvements and Misbehaviors in Mutual Funds

I show that after an ESG score improvement, mutual fund managers become more likely to engage in window dressing and reducing reporting small losses. These patterns are consistent with a moral-licensing mechanism in which recent prosocial behavior can license less prosocial behavior thereafter. The evidence is inconsistent with the explanations based on pecuniary benefits and further test rules out performance pressure explanation.
